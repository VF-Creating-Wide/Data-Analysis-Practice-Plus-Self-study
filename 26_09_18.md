# The Psychology of a Man Who Fears Nothing  一个无所畏惧之人的心理学

Courage is the specific quality of a man who feels fear and acts anyway.  
勇气是一个感到恐惧却依然行动的人所特有的品质。

The man who has to summon courage has already lost the interior battle.  
一个不得不鼓起勇气的人，已经输掉了内心的战斗

The fear has arrived. He is now managing it. 

Managing fear is exhausting.

It works sometimes, it fails often, and it leaves the man depleted afterwards because every act of courage costs something psychologically that must be recovered.

There is another possibility that modern culture has almost entirely forgotten.

It is possibility of the man who dose not need courage because the fear does not arise.

It is not rhetorical distinction, it is a psychological one, and the specific mechanism by which some rare men achieve this state has been documented across history, but almost never explained clearly.
